Ask any AI to count the R's in the word strawberry. Watch what happens. It confidently tells you there are two.
Why does an advanced AI fail at basic counting?
Because the model doesn't see letters. It sees tokens.
Tokens are chunks of text the AI learned by scanning the internet.
A short, common word is a single token.
A longer word gets chopped up into pieces.
Even emojis are their own tokens.
This chunking happens through byte-pair encoding, merging common letter pairs into bigger blocks.
But this creates a massive language tax.
Take the exact same sentence in English, Hindi, and Korean.
English is cheap. But Hindi and Korean cost three times more tokens for the exact same meaning.
So, back to strawberry. Why does it break?
The model saw the tokens 'straw' and 'berry'. The R's were trapped inside those chunks the whole time, and the model can't look inside them.
You speak words. It hears tokens.
